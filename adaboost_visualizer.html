AdaBoost Algorithm Information

AdaBoost (Adaptive Boosting) is an ensemble learning method that combines multiple weak classifiers to create a strong classifier:

  1. Initialize all sample weights equally (1/N)
  2. Train a weak classifier on weighted samples
  3. Calculate classifier error and weight (α)
  4. Increase weights for misclassified samples
  5. Decrease weights for correctly classified samples
  6. Repeat for specified number of iterations
  7. Final prediction is weighted vote of all classifiers

α (alpha) = 0.5 * ln((1 - error) / error) - Higher α means more confident classifier

Weight Update: w = w * exp(α * incorrect) - Misclassified samples get exponentially higher weights
